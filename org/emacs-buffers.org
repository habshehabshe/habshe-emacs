Buffer-local display modes, globalized: line numbers, current-line
highlight, and soft wrapping. Soft wrapping wraps at word boundaries
(visual-line-mode implies word-wrap buffer-locally, so no explicit
word-wrap is needed) and produces no fringe continuation arrows.
Continuation arrows are their own fringe indicator — the continuation entry
of fringe-indicator-alist — drawn on ordinary continued lines whenever
visual-line-mode is off; they are not truncation artifacts.
visual-line-mode suppresses them per buffer (visual-line-fringe-indicators,
default (nil nil), draws nothing on wrapped lines), and it disables line
truncation anyway, so no arrow of either kind can appear.

The fill-column indicator and its interaction with visual-line-mode
will be designed and added here in a later pass.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp :tangle "../lisp/my-emacs-buffers.el"
  ;;; my-emacs-buffers.el --- GENERATED from org/emacs-buffers.org — do not edit -*- lexical-binding: t; -*-
  ;;; Commentary: Buffer display: line numbers, highlight, wrapping.

  (defgroup my-emacs-buffers nil
    "Buffer display for my Emacs configuration.")

  ;; Line numbers in every buffer.
  (global-display-line-numbers-mode 1)

  ;; Highlight the line point is on.
  (global-hl-line-mode 1)

  ;; Soft-wrap long lines at word boundaries. No mid-word breaks, no
  ;; truncation, and no continuation arrows on wrapped lines.
  (global-visual-line-mode 1)

  (provide 'my-emacs-buffers)
#+end_src
